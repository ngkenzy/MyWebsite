Operating Framework

Three Focuses

These are the reminders behind the notes: do not cling, accept change, and act cleanly. Edit this section anytime as your thinking becomes clearer.

Tín Tâm Minh

Do not cling. Do not resist. Let the mind settle before choosing a response.

Vô Thường

Everything changes. Hold plans lightly and meet each season as it comes.

Bát Chánh Đạo

Act rightly. Live cleanly. Let daily conduct be the practice.

Five Pillars

Communication

Clear, calm, brief. Practice simple stories, better listening, and words that reduce friction.

Thinking

Solve real problems only. Build useful technical skill, study consistently, and close open loops.

Health

Maintain the body. Walk, hike, lift, play, and keep the mind steady through practice.

Finances

Stay disciplined. No emotion, no chasing, no panic. Protect freedom through steady decisions.

Family

Be present, patient, and supportive. Guide without gripping. Love without rushing.
