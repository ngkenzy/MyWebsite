About

Technical depth without losing the decision.

I am an Operations Research Analyst and military officer with deep experience applying analytics, modeling, intelligence, and data systems to difficult operational problems. My work sits at the intersection of quantitative reasoning, software, and decision support.

I am also the founder and builder of SOLPIENT Research, a point-in-time fundamental investment research system designed to preserve what the research believed at each date and compare later evidence against prior assumptions.
